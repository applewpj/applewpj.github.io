- `ICLR 2027 Under Review` **Scalable Data Selection via Language-Model Distribution Resampling**, **Pingjie Wang**, Zechen Hu, et al.

- `NeurIPS 2026 Under Review` [Smooth Scaling Laws Hide Stepwise Token Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29858), **Pingjie Wang**<sup>*</sup>, Zechen Hu<sup>*</sup>, Peiru Yang<sup>*</sup>, Fu Guo, Debing Zhang. [**Code**](https://github.com/applewpj/token-learning-spectrum) \

  We introduce NTK-Selector, a Jacobian-free NTK approximation for selecting transferable general-domain data for low-resource adaptation.
